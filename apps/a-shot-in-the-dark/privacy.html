A Shot in the Dark

Privacy policy

Your photos stay under your control.

The app

A Shot in the Dark (com.sebastienvanblaere.shotinthedark) is made by Sebastien Vanblaere, Ghent, Belgium. The app does not send your photos, videos, settings or usage information to the developer. It has no internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.

Camera, audio and permissions

The app uses CAMERA to capture photos and video, and VIBRATE for haptic feedback. It does not request microphone access. Videos are recorded without audio.

What stays on your device

Photos and videos are saved to your device's media library in Pictures/ShotInTheDark using Android MediaStore. Viewfinder position and size, brightness and volume key settings are stored in the app's private storage using Android DataStore.

You can delete photos and videos through your gallery or file manager. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes its local settings; media in your shared library may remain. We have no server copy of your media or settings and cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

Backups and other apps

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for app settings. Whether they are backed up depends on your device and system settings. Your gallery, cloud photo service or other apps with media access may also sync your photos and videos. Those services are separate from A Shot in the Dark and follow their own settings and privacy policies.

These web pages and downloads

The app pages do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting provider receives network information, such as your IP address and the requested page or file, to deliver pages and APK downloads. These pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also makes requests to Google. The Android app does not make these website requests.

Photography, permission and GDPR

The venue and the creators always have the final say. If photography or filming is not permitted, this app does not change that. Respect the privacy and rights of the people you photograph or film.

Recognisable people in photos or videos may be personal data. You are responsible for your own recording, use and sharing of images, and for meeting applicable privacy, image rights and GDPR requirements. A dark screen or local storage does not remove those responsibilities. Purely personal or household use may be exempt from the GDPR; do not assume that public sharing or professional use is covered by that exemption. European Commission: when the GDPR applies.

Contact and changes

If you email us, your address and message are used to reply. Please do not send private photos or videos with a support request.
