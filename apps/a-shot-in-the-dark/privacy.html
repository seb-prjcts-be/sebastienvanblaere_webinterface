Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

a shot in the dark collects no data.

a shot in the dark (com.sebastienvanblaere.shotinthedark) does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It is technically unable to make network connections.
  • There is no account, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking code.
  • The only permissions requested are CAMERA (to capture photos and silent video) and VIBRATE (for haptic feedback when a photo or video starts or stops). The app does not request microphone access and does not record audio.
  • Photos and videos you take are written only to your own device, in Pictures/ShotInTheDark, via Android's standard MediaStore.
  • Your settings (viewfinder position, viewfinder size, brightness, and volume key assignment) are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore and never leave the phone, except through standard Android system backups if enabled on your device.

This website (sebastienvanblaere.be) uses no analytics or tracking cookies. Standard HTTP server logs are processed by the hosting provider to serve the pages and downloads.
