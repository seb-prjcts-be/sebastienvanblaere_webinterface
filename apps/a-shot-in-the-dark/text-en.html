A Shot in the Dark

Black-screen camera for concerts and theatre. Photos and video via volume keys.

Oops, I lit up the whole theatre again!

Light shows belong on stage, not in the audience.

You want one photo of that concert. Out comes your phone, and suddenly you're putting on a light show of your own.

A Shot in the Dark is an Android camera for concerts, theatre and live performance. A black screen, a small dimmable viewfinder and a volume key for the shot. Take a memory home with consideration for the performance and the people around you.

Set it up before the show

Choose the position, size and brightness of the viewfinder, or turn the viewfinder off completely! A real shot in the dark.

Controls you can feel

Press a volume key once to take a photo, or hold it to start or stop a video*. Vibration confirms your actions. You can swap the functions of the volume keys.

Lock the camera to prevent accidental shots, screen interactions or unwelcome welcome screens. The camera turns off. The screen stays black, with a small red lock indicator. Hold the lock key to unlock the camera and take photos again.

*Videos are recorded without audio at the creators' request.

Simple and private

No ads, accounts or tracking.
The app has no internet access.
Photos and videos are saved locally on your own phone. The viewfinder size does not affect recording quality.

For Android 10 and later. Use the app where photography and filming are permitted.

Inspired by the simplicity of the old Agfamatic: look through a viewfinder, press a button and capture the moment.

Created by Sebastien Vanblaere, an artist, creative coding teacher and concertgoer from Ghent, Belgium. For that one photo, without becoming the light show.
