Install & update +

This is a direct Android download, available here while Google Play publication is pending. There is no iPhone version.

The dark screen works while this app is open. It does not dim your phone's home screen or other apps. Set it up before the room goes dark; the volume-button lock controls the app, not your phone's screen lock.

  1. Download the APK (the app's installation file) on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to install this app.
  3. Open the app and allow camera access. Take a test photo and check that it saves before the show.

Afterwards, you can turn off your browser's permission to install apps. If Android blocks installation, read the warning and keep general security protections enabled. Android installation guidance.

For updates, return here and install the latest APK over the existing app. An older development build may have a different signing key. Check that your media is safely stored before removing it; reinstalling may reset settings.

Your photos, your phone +

No account, no analytics, no internet permission. The app saves photos and silent videos in Pictures/ShotInTheDark. It does not record microphone audio.

Your gallery or backup service may sync your media; Android may back up app settings. This page has no analytics or tracking cookies. The hosting provider processes network requests.

Beta & release details +

Version 1.0, released 16 September 2026. First public beta; tested in an Android emulator, not yet widely tested on physical phones. Check photo and video saving on your own device before relying on it at a performance.
