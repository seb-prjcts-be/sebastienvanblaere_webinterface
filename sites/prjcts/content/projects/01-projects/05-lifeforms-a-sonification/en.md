<h2>brunk</h2>
<p>Bert and I studied together at the academy in Ghent. We quickly became good friends. 
The most magical moments always occurred when Bert found a guitar laying around and started doodling. The sounds always seemed to go straight to my heart.
</p>
<p>brunks music, with a small b, is perhaps best described in terms of contrasts.<br>
Almost dichotomies, really.</p>
<p>Extreme, yet careful.<br>Acoustic, yet sampled.<br>Epicly cinematic, or small and domestic.<br>Deafening, but very present in silence.<br>Breathtakingly beautiful. Agonizingly painful.</p>
<p>Bert is clearly not afraid of the extremes, but also remains particularly masterful in all the beautiful nuances in between.
<br>Wallow in the beauty of his music, but always expect a dissonance.
</p>
