<p><b>Recursion</b></p>
<p>Recursion is an incredibly powerful principle. You ask the computer to do something in a defined area, a <b>function</b>. eg. draw a square. Then you refer to itself a few times in the function with other parameters. This gives you fractals, which continue until you stop the process. View the principle in the example below.<br><br>
