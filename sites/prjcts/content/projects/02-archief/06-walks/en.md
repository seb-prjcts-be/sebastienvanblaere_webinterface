<p><b>Random walkers</b></p>
<p>A random walker is an object that can take one step in either direction, depending on the results of a virtual dice throw. 1 is up, 2 is down, etc.<br>The random walkers in this project are forced to walk mostly to the right.<br>When a walker arrives to the right, a unique image is downloaded to your computer.</p>
