<p>Ever since I was a kid, I was attracted to photography.<br>
I pointed my parents' cameras, first the Kodak, then the Polaroid, at curiosities, anomalies, nobody else had noticed.</p>

<p>When I was eleven my dad got me my very own <b>Kodak Ektra</b>.<br> 
It came as a beautiful yellow package with a black storage box, a 16mm 110 film cassette and a silver chain to make sure you wouldn't ever drop the precious apparatus.</p>    

<p>Since that time, my cameras have grown bigger and better.</p>
<p>Holding the first digital camera in my hands (the schools' <b>Sony Mavica</b> with 3.2MB diskette storage), I felt a shiver down my spine. Everything was about to change (again).<br> 
And so it did.</p> 

<p>Now, I mostly walk the streets with my <b>Sony Alpha</b>.<br><br> The joy of being fully aware of my surroundings, when holding a camera is priceless. 
<br>Behind every step rests the promise of a new discovery, a light, a person, an image. 
<br>Hyperfocus is the state I prefer to be in.</p>
